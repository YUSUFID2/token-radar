How the ranking works

implied = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity × category × chain × likelihood

Every row is scored by the same function of the snapshot’s own figures: TVL, 7-day growth (clamped −60% to +120%, weight 0.5), age since first listing (maturity 0.6–1.0 over 3 years), a category multiplier, a chain multiplier (up to 1.10 for multi-chain protocols) and a launch likelihood (0.40–1.00 from age, growth and category). Category multipliers: Risk Curators 1.25, Onchain Capital Allocator 1.20, Derivatives 1.15, Dexs 1.10, Lending 1.05, CDP 1.00, RWA 0.95, Yield 0.90, Yield Aggregator 0.85, Payments 0.80, other 1.00.

“No token” means the protocol records no token in this snapshot — never a claim that it has none. Implied value is this radar’s own estimate, not a price and not investment advice. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are excluded from the board entirely.
